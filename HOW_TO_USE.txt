# HOW TO USE JOBFLOW AI®
================================================================================
JobFlow AI® — End-to-End AI Job Application & Recruitment Intelligence Platform
Version: v1.2.0

================================================================================
1. PLATFORM OVERVIEW & VALUE PROPOSITION
================================================================================
JobFlow AI® is a full-stack, AI-powered platform designed to streamline both 
ends of the hiring process: career management for job seekers and candidate 
sourcing for recruiters.

It integrates:
- Next.js 14 React Minimalist Editorial Frontend
- Node.js Express API Gateway with MongoDB Atlas Cloud Persistence
- Python FastAPI Microservice for TF-IDF Cosine Match Vectors & NLP Parsing
- Stripe Payment Gateway with 2 Free Trial Searches & $29/mo Pro Pass


================================================================================
2. WHO IS JOBFLOW AI HELPFUL FOR?
================================================================================

[A] JOB SEEKERS & SOFTWARE ENGINEERS
------------------------------------
* Common Challenges:
  - Resumes get silently rejected by automated Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS).
  - Writing customized cover letters for dozens of applications is time-consuming.
  - Uncertainty about which skills match a job posting vs. what keywords are missing.
  - Nervousness during technical and behavioral interviews.

* How JobFlow AI Helps:
  1. AI Resume Parser & ATS Score Gauge: Instantly analyzes plain text/PDF 
     resumes, gives a 0-100 ATS readiness score, extracts skills, and provides 
     actionable formatting recommendations.
  2. Cosine Vector Job Matcher: Compares your resume against job postings using 
     scikit-learn TF-IDF vector math to show exact matched skills vs missing keywords.
  3. 1-Click Tailored Cover Letters: Synthesizes high-converting cover letters 
     customized for the target company and role requirements in seconds.
  4. Kanban Application Pipeline Tracker: Organizes applications across 5 stages 
     (Saved, Applied, Interviewing, Offer Received, Closed) with cloud state persistence.
  5. Interactive AI Mock Interviewer: Generates targeted technical/behavioral 
     questions for your specific job role and evaluates typed answers (score 1-10 
     plus improvement tips).


[B] RECRUITERS, HR MANAGERS & HIRING TEAMS
------------------------------------------
* Common Challenges:
  - Spending hours manually reviewing hundreds of unranked candidate resumes.
  - Difficulty drafting detailed technical job descriptions quickly.
  - Fragmented candidate tracking across spreadsheets.

* How JobFlow AI Helps:
  1. AI Job Requisition Generator: Auto-generates comprehensive technical job 
     descriptions with required skill tags in 1 click.
  2. Automated Candidate Ranking: Automatically ranks applicants by AI Match 
     Score percentage, highlighting top-tier candidates immediately.
  3. Centralized Candidate Pipeline: Track applicant progression from screening to 
     final offer.


[C] FREELANCERS & CONTRACT CONSULTANTS
--------------------------------------
* How JobFlow AI Helps:
  - Quickly tailor proposals and resumes for multiple client requisitions to 
    maximize acceptance rates.

3. STEP-BY-STEP USER GUIDE
================================================================================

FOR JOB SEEKERS:
----------------
Step 1: Open the Seeker Portal at http://localhost:3000/seeker
Step 2: Go to "Resume AI" -> Paste your resume text -> Click "Parse Resume".
        Review your ATS score, extracted skill tags, and formatting feedback.
Step 3: Go to "AI Job Matcher" -> Browse active job postings.
        Click "AI Cosine Match" to see your match percentage and missing keywords.
Step 4: Click "1-Click Apply" to add the job to your Kanban tracker.
Step 5: Go to "Cover Letter" -> Select the target job -> Click "Generate Cover Letter".
Step 6: Go to "AI Interview" -> Select your target job -> Click "Start Practice".
        Answer the questions and click "Evaluate Response" to get AI scoring (1-10).

FOR RECRUITERS:
---------------
Step 1: Open the Recruiter Hub at http://localhost:3000/recruiter
Step 2: Click "Post New AI Job Requisition".
Step 3: Enter Job Title -> Click "Auto-Generate with AI" to generate the description.
Step 4: Click "Publish Requisition" to make it live for candidates.
Step 5: View the "AI-Ranked Applicants" column to inspect top-matching candidates.
